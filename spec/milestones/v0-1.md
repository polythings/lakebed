# 0.1: Iceberg for Timelake and lakeglass

**In progress**

The first release: the store, clock, and random traits, the Iceberg crate, and the `object_store` adapter, enough for
Timelake's WASM reader and writer to use lakekit instead of their own Iceberg code, and for lakeglass to read Iceberg
tables in the browser.

|---:|---|---|
| 1 | [Store, Clock, and Random](../0001-store-clock-random.md) | Done |
| 2 | [One crate per format](../0002-formats.md) | In progress |
| 3 | [Apache Iceberg](../0003-iceberg.md) | In progress |
| 4 | [Store adapters](../0004-store-adapters.md) | In progress |

**Done when:** the crates are on crates.io ([#7](https://github.com/polythings/lakekit/issues/7)), Timelake depends
on them by version, and lakeglass's Iceberg reader is built on them.
